About DecentCanopy

DecentCanopy helps communities see the projects, people, funds, and relationships that make a resilient creative ecosystem. Follow the connections, explore their sources, and find the wider forest around each endeavor.

This canopy brings together Artizen's public project graph and a separately curated Green Tea Party cluster. Artizen records are shown as published; locally curated connections are identified as such.

Decent means connecting geographically distributed endeavors to public ledgers and transparent evidence. Opt in to local creator profiles, website links, public wallet links, and locations on our globe. Replay participant-reported boosts and purchases, and explore season-stamped funding.

Public wallet balances are read only on request. A wallet link does not verify Artizen identity or ownership; imported activity is not a live or verified Artizen feed. Choose country, city, precise coordinates, or a symbolic space marker. Nothing you import is automatically uploaded or pinned to IPFS.

Creator cards now preview an Artizen account space: projects, the funds those projects touch, reported stewardship, and each person's and place's own location. Artizen sign-in and artifact collections are coming soon; we will never ask for your Artizen password.

See a filled-in canopy on TheJollyLaMa's creator blip: profile picture, project artwork and websites, the Decent Agency Discord, dated totals from public Artizen pages, projects they support, recent boosts, and pending fund submissions (a submission is not membership), all labeled with their source.

New here? The 🧭 Tour walks you through the map and lets you choose your own view. Sources and limits live in the ⓘ Data notes tab.

Open source and community-built. Ideas and fixes start as GitHub issues; contributors earn on the auto payroll.

  2. Pitch ideas or pick up issues. bounty labels pay in ART or USDC; the idea's author shares 20% via idea-credit.
  3. Ship it. Merged work is queued on the payroll automatically and paid out on Base.

Grow your local canopy

Opt in to preview and import data you are permitted to use. This prototype stores it only in this browser, not on a server or IPFS. Do not include passwords, cookies, private contact lists, or another person's private data.

Artizen sign-in is not integrated. Never enter your Artizen credentials here. Imported identities, ownership, websites, and activity are unverified participant claims.

Wallet connection is optional and does not verify Artizen identity or card ownership.

Preview an import

Use our versioned JSON format—not an arbitrary Artizen export. Download the template and consult the import documentation. All activity is historical until an approved live source is connected.
