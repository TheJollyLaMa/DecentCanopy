About DecentCanopy

DecentCanopy helps communities see the projects, people, funds, and relationships that make a resilient creative ecosystem. Follow the connections, explore their sources, and find the wider forest around each endeavor.

A home for independent creators to find each other, document their projects, and grow new connections. The existing Artizen project and fund canopy is a frozen historical archive, alongside the Green Tea Party cluster. No new data is expected from Artizen.

Decent means connecting geographically distributed endeavors to public ledgers and transparent evidence. Connect a wallet to create your own Decent Creator blip. Edit your profile, record project progress, and track funding sources you are exploring, applying to, or reporting receipts from.

Your wallet signs your public updates. Signing costs no gas and sends no payment. Profiles are portable IPFS records; a public repository index helps everyone discover them for now. This is not fully decentralized discovery yet. Signatures prove wallet control, not real-world identity or the truth of a claim.

If you want, document your Artizen experience in your journey: what happened, your own payout experience, and evidence you choose to share. We do not assert that any payouts were or were not made. These accounts remain separate from the historical archive, and are not independently verified.

See a filled-in canopy on TheJollyLaMa's creator blip: profile picture, project artwork and websites, the Decent Agency Discord, dated totals from public Artizen pages, projects they support, recent boosts, and pending fund submissions (a submission is not membership), all labeled with their source.

New here? The 🧭 Tour walks you through the map and lets you choose your own view. Sources and limits live in the ⓘ Data notes tab.

🌱 Build the canopy with us

Open source and community-built. Ideas and fixes start as GitHub issues; contributors earn on the auto payroll.

  1. File an issue to join the contributor whitelist.
  2. Pitch ideas or pick up issues. Funded bounty labels specify USDC; the idea's author shares 20% via idea-credit.
  3. Ship it. Merged work is queued on the payroll automatically and paid out on Base.

📌 Keep the canopy available

  • No token needed to join. Use your wallet and the community pinning account to publish, or export your signed record to keep independently.
  • $0.10 USDC per qualifying week. Keep the canopy backup pinned using Pinata or IPFS Desktop/Kubo and serve it through your own public gateway. Approval and matching-byte checks are required; at most 20 pinners are rewarded each week. A queued reward is not a payment; settlement depends on available USDC funding.

Claims are signed with your wallet right here. Signing is free, sends no transaction, and needs no GitHub account.

📌 Pin the canopy yourself

Every copy makes the community dataset harder to lose. Pin the latest backup:

No backup CID published yet

With your own IPFS node (Kubo)

ipfs pin add <cid>

With IPFS Desktop

Open Files → Import → From IPFS, paste the backup CID, and retain the pin. For reward checks, expose a public HTTPS gateway serving your pinned data; a desktop pin without a reachable gateway cannot qualify yet.

With your Pinata account

Use a scoped Pinata key and revoke it afterwards if you like. DecentCanopy never stores your key.

$0.10 USDC per qualifying week

Serve your pin from your own public gateway and sign up below. After approval, a weekly matching-byte check queues USDC to your connected wallet on Base, subject to the 20-pinner weekly cap. This shows availability, not proof of independent storage. Queued is not paid; settlement depends on USDC funding and an authorized transaction.
