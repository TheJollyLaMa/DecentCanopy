## DecentAgency project and repository map

Open the [DecentAgency map](https://thejollylama.github.io/DecentCanopy/?canopy=agency)
for the curated library inventory: Canopy and Busking now, BigNuten next.
Projects are distinct from repositories. The project/repo index supports keyboard navigation;
cards explain purpose, next steps, sources, and directed relationships.
Observed, steward-reported, proposed, and unresolved connections are labeled separately.
This is a dated planning snapshot, not GitHub authorization, live issue synchronization,
proof of ownership, or proof of payouts.
See the [map guide and maintenance instructions](../README.md#decentagency-mapping-our-current-work).

## Three different kinds of evidence

- **Archive:** a fixed public snapshot, not live Artizen data or proof of payouts.
- **Signed creator record:** proves the signing wallet controls an update, not that every claim is true.
- **Payroll ledger:** pending is queued; paid means a confirmed on-chain payout; settled means that payment has also been recorded in the repository.
